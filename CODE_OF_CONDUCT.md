# Code of Conduct

GPT Auto Resume follows the Contributor Covenant spirit: be respectful, constructive, and careful with user privacy.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and specific in feedback.
- Keep bug reports actionable.
- Respect privacy and security boundaries.
- Do not ask users to share cookies, tokens, passwords, or private conversation contents.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Publishing private diagnostics without consent.
- Encouraging unsafe automation that bypasses the app safety model.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, or contributions that violate these expectations.
